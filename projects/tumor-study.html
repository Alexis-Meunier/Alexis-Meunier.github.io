Tumor Progression Study

Compares two brain MRI scans of the same patient, taken at different dates, to measure how a tumor changed.

Python, ITK, VTK. Source on GitHub

Overview

A group project with ITK and VTK. The second scan is first aligned on the first one, then the tumor is segmented in both, so the two masks can be compared in the same space.

Features

  • Registration in two stages, rigid then affine, with Mattes mutual information
  • Tumor segmentation by confidence-connected region growing
  • Volume change between the two dates
  • 2D slice viewer with both tumor contours drawn on it
  • 3D view of both tumors, and volume ray-casting of the brain
