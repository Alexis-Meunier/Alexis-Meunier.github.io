Deferred Renderer

An OpenGL renderer extended step by step with culling, a depth pre-pass, shadow maps and deferred shading.

C++17, OpenGL 4.5, GLSL. Source on GitHub

Overview

Built during the OM3D course at EPITA. The course gives a small OpenGL engine that loads glTF scenes, and each lab adds a rendering technique to it. The test scenes include Amazon Lumberyard Bistro and the Damaged Helmet.

Features

  • Frustum culling and back-face culling with bounding boxes
  • Depth pre-pass to skip shading hidden pixels
  • Shadow mapping for the sun, with shadow acne removed
  • Deferred shading with a G-buffer
  • Point lights drawn in a separate lighting pass over the G-buffer
